Chapter 01 — The stakes

Nine in ten Ohio students learn in a public school.

Regardless of race, background, or ZIP code

Chapter 02 — What we believe

Seven commitments to every student.

Great public schools shape the future of our state and embody our shared values. Here is what we fight to protect.

Keep public resources in public schools

Vouchers route public dollars into unaccountable private schools. We fully fund what makes public schools great — for the 90%, not the few.

Quality education for all

Equitable funding that attracts great educators, enables small classes, and delivers a well‑rounded curriculum.

Early education opportunities

Expanding Pre‑K so students build the groundwork for lifelong learning from the very start.

Safe schools

Healthy, supportive environments that protect the wellbeing of students and educators alike.

Support for students & educators

Ending educator shortages and delivering the one‑on‑one support students actually need.

A great education in every ZIP code

From the lake shore to the banks of the Ohio, every child deserves an excellent public school.

Local leadership

Elevating the educators, administrators, bus drivers, and staff who keep our schools running and safe.
